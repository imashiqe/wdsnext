# SEO strategy for Web Development Service USA

## Positioning and primary goal

**Audience:** US business owners and marketing decision-makers looking to hire a developer or agency.

**Primary conversion:** qualified project inquiries through `/get-quote`, WhatsApp, or contact.

**Primary topic:** business website development, supported by technology-, use case-, and solution-specific service pages.

SEO is an acquisition program, not a ranking switch. These pages provide a technical starting point. Rankings depend on demand, competition, genuinely helpful content, credible experience, links, and performance over time.

## Technical launch priorities (first 2 weeks)

1. Confirm preferred host and DNS; enforce HTTPS and a single `www` or non-`www` canonical hostname.
2. If rebuilding an existing site, inventory all URLs, organic entry pages, and backlinks; map 301/308 redirects and validate no important page is lost.
3. Deploy the Next.js site and inspect server-generated HTML for service pages and article pages.
4. Check `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, meta robots, canonical tags, and generated 404s.
5. Create/verify Google Search Console domain property. Submit XML sitemap. Add Bing Webmaster Tools if desired.
6. Add analytics with consent and privacy requirements considered. Track meaningful conversions, not only page views.
7. Confirm forms deliver and notification email is authenticated (SPF/DKIM); test WhatsApp links.
8. Run mobile Lighthouse and real-world performance checks and fix high-impact loading issues.

## Content and authority (weeks 3–8)

- Add verifiable case studies: business problem, scope, technologies, work completed, and real before/after evidence. Only cite metrics with documented measurement and permission.
- Publish detailed comparisons or technical guides that answer actual buyer questions, e.g. Shopify vs custom store, website migration planning, choosing a web development company, what belongs in a web development RFP.
- Add team expertise, delivery process, real legal/business identity details, and verified contact methods.
- Strengthen topic clusters by linking helpful insights to their relevant commercial service pages, and services back to informative resources.
- Validate services offered and remove capabilities that are not delivered.

## Optimization and acquisition (weeks 9–12)

- Review Google Search Console query, impression, CTR, and indexing data by page, country, and device.
- Improve pages with relevant impressions but weak engagement or missing topical coverage; avoid keyword stuffing.
- Test hero positioning and quote-form completion paths.
- Pursue relevant business mentions, partner referrals, and editorial links where appropriate; do not buy spam links.
- Expand into industry or region pages only when there is genuine expertise, materially different helpful content, and real geographic service relevance.

## KPIs

- Indexed, canonical pages and crawl errors
- Impressions and clicks for *relevant, non-branded* commercial queries
- Qualified inquiries from organic search
- Lead-to-discovery and proposal-to-close rates
- Landing-page engagement and form completion rate
- Field Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) where data is available
- Content production quality and refresh cadence

## Do not do

- No fake USA office, fabricated reviews, invented rankings or client claims.
- No thousands of AI-generated near-duplicate pages or doorway city pages.
- No unverified “#1” superlatives or promise of first-page rankings.
- No blind redesign that drops indexed URLs without redirects.
- No structured data describing services, businesses, or reviews that do not actually exist.
